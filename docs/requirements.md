# EnergiTrack — Requirements

## Project Objective

EnergiTrack is a PostgreSQL database for managing and analyzing energy consumption and production activities in a fictional steel manufacturing plant.

## Functional Requirements

The system should be able to:

* Manage production units.
* Manage industrial equipment and their specifications.
* Manage manufactured products.
* Record hourly production quantities and scrap.
* Record raw material consumption.
* Record hourly energy consumption for each equipment.
* Support electricity and natural gas as energy sources.
* Manage energy tariffs over time.
* Calculate energy consumption costs.
* Record equipment maintenance operations.
* Calculate equipment downtime when required.
* Analyze energy consumption and energy efficiency.

## Data Analysis Requirements

The database should provide structured data that can later be used for:

* Energy consumption analysis
* Energy efficiency analysis
* Energy consumption prediction
* Energy anomaly detection

## Scope

The project does not include:

* Inventory management
* Purchasing and suppliers
* Employee management
* Accounting
* Detailed metallurgical information
